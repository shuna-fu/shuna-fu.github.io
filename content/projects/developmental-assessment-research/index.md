This project developed an evidence-based developmental competency framework to guide the design of the **Dream Power** curriculum for primary school students in China. Drawing on developmental psychology, literature synthesis, and qualitative research, the project established grade-specific developmental rubrics (Grades 1–6) with four developmental performance levels that served as the theoretical foundation for curriculum design and implementation.

## Status

Completed (2022)

## Role

Research Project Lead

## Organization

Shanghai Adream Charitable Foundation, China

## Research Questions

What developmental competencies and milestones characterize purpose discovery, dream formulation, and goal pursuit across primary school development?

## My Contributions

- Led the development and validation of an evidence-based developmental competency framework for primary school students (Grades 1–6).
- Defined age-specific developmental competencies and four-level developmental performance descriptors based on developmental psychology theory and empirical evidence.
- Conducted comprehensive literature reviews on child development, motivation, and social-emotional learning.
- Designed and conducted semi-structured interviews with primary and secondary school educators.
- Synthesized theoretical and qualitative evidence to identify developmental milestones and competency indicators.
- Developed grade-specific developmental rubrics that served as the theoretical foundation for the Dream Power curriculum.
- Collaborated with interdisciplinary teams to translate developmental science into scalable educational practice.

## Research Impact

The developmental competency framework served as the theoretical foundation for the **Dream Power** curriculum, which has been implemented in schools serving underserved communities across rural China and has reached approximately **1.2 million** primary school students.
