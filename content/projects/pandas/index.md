This project investigates the structure and development of wisdom across Chinese and Canadian populations. The research aims to improve the measurement of wisdom and examine its relationships with psychological well-being and related psychological constructs using cross-cultural data.

## Status

Current (2024–Present)

## Role

Research Project Manager

## Lab

Wisdom and Identity Lab, University of Toronto

## Research Question

How does wisdom develop across cultures, and what psychological and cultural factors are associated with wisdom and psychological well-being?

## Research Methods

- Cross-cultural research
- Survey research
- Psychometric analysis
- Quantitative analysis
