This longitudinal experimental study investigates whether wisdom can be cultivated through a structured two-chair dialogue intervention. By following participants over time, the project examines how reflective practices and everyday life experiences contribute to the development of wisdom, wise reasoning, and psychological well-being.

## Status

Current (2025–Present)

## Role

Research Project Manager

## Lab

Wisdom and Identity Lab, University of Toronto

## Research Questions

- Can wisdom be cultivated through structured reflective practices and a two-chair dialogue intervention?
- How do everyday life experiences contribute to the development of wisdom and wise reasoning?
- What are the underlying psychological mechanisms through which the intervention promotes wisdom development?
- How do the effectiveness and mechanisms of the intervention differ between Chinese and Canadian university students?

## Research Methods

- Longitudinal experimental study
- Repeated-measures design
- Two-chair dialogue intervention
- Mixed-methods research
- Quantitative and qualitative analysis
